    RESULT
    Program the target, press RUN. Wrong runs get a worked explanation here.
    COMP RULE — LEFT / RIGHT / CENTER
    Walk the cut from BEGIN to END. LEFT = tool center offset one tool radius to your left, RIGHT = to your right, CENTER = tool center rides the programmed line. On a profile, comp so the cut edge lands on the line with the tool over the waste side.
    BOLT-HOLE MATH
    Hole i of N on radius R about (XC,YC), first hole at START°, angles CCW from +X:
    θ = START + i·360/N
    x = XC + R·cos θ · y = YC + R·sin θ
    One BOLT HOLE event drills all N — or compute each and DRILL them yourself.
    CLIMB VS CONVENTIONAL (M3 SPINDLE)
    The spindle turns clockwise seen from above (M3). Climb milling puts the material on the RIGHT of your direction of travel — outside profile: walk the part CW with comp LEFT. Material on the left = conventional (outside profile CCW + comp RIGHT cuts the very same edge, just conventional). Scoring here is by geometry; the result panel tells you which way you actually cut.
    CONRAD
    A corner radius blended between this cut event and the NEXT one — the control trims both moves back and inserts a tangent arc, radius = CONRAD. Needs the next event to start where this one ends. Too big for the sides → it gets clamped and you are told.
